My friends and I had been trying to plan a group trip to Cedar Point for a few years now since it has some of the, if not the best rollercoasters in the country/world.

drove up 4.5 hours day before and spent overnight in an airbnb with 11 people and spent the whole next day at the park.

rode all of the best rides there multiple times - millenium force was amazing, super fast, big drop and didn't have any of the nauseating feeling that some of the other rides like steel vengeance or maverick gave me.

the trip home was a little rough, ended up switching with a friend to drive home since i was exhausted

10/10 experience
